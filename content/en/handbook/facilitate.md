As a facilitation team, walk through these five core delivery questions before your event:

## Question 4: Are You Prepared for Facilitation Challenges?

Because artificial intelligence touches on personal fears, sensational news, and political divides, five common challenges frequently arise:

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border-inline-start: 4px solid var(--ct-color-cerulean, #40A9FF); border-radius: 0 10px 10px 0; padding: 1.25rem 1.5rem;">
    <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.4rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C); font-size: 1.05rem;">1. Keeping on Topic &amp; The 'Parking Lot'</h4>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      AI is broad, and participants often raise important issues that fall outside the planned agenda. Dedicate a flip-chart sheet as a <strong>"Parking Lot"</strong>. Write down valuable tangential topics immediately, validate them, and review them at the end of the day or suggest them for follow-up assemblies.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border-inline-start: 4px solid var(--ct-color-cerulean, #40A9FF); border-radius: 0 10px 10px 0; padding: 1.25rem 1.5rem;">
    <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.4rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C); font-size: 1.05rem;">2. Addressing Misinformation, Sci-Fi Tropes &amp; Metaphors</h4>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      Participants may conflate current machine learning with conscious Hollywood robots, or cite unverified viral rumors. Acknowledge underlying emotional concerns (e.g., loss of agency) while gently anchoring the room in current, real-world technologies using the toolkit's factual backgrounders.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border-inline-start: 4px solid var(--ct-color-coral, #FF5F6F); border-radius: 0 10px 10px 0; padding: 1.25rem 1.5rem;">
    <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.4rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C); font-size: 1.05rem;">3. Managing Dominant Voices &amp; Technologists</h4>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      You may have participants with strong tech backgrounds or passionate "pro" vs "anti" stances who dominate discussion. Confidently intervene: <em>“Thank you for that perspective. Let’s make sure we hear from others around the table who haven't spoken yet.”</em> Rearrange small breakout groups between sessions if dynamics become unbalanced.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border-inline-start: 4px solid var(--ct-color-coral, #FF5F6F); border-radius: 0 10px 10px 0; padding: 1.25rem 1.5rem;">
    <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.4rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C); font-size: 1.05rem;">4. Maintaining Emotional Safety &amp; Safeguarding</h4>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      Discussing personal experiences with AI may surface sensitive disclosures (e.g. relying on chatbots for mental health crises, non-consensual deepfakes, job loss, or existential dread). Establish upfront that participants can step out anytime. Provide contact details for local mental health support, and establish safeguarding procedures if working with minors or vulnerable adults.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border-inline-start: 4px solid var(--ct-color-reef-teal, #00D8B4); border-radius: 0 10px 10px 0; padding: 1.25rem 1.5rem;">
    <h4 style="margin: 0 0 0.4rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C); font-size: 1.05rem;">5. Respecting Personal Data &amp; AI Usage Views</h4>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">

## Question 5: How Will You Evaluate Your Assembly?

Evaluation ensures continuous learning and proves community impact:

1. **Participant Exit Slips (5 mins):** Distribute a brief 3-question survey at the close of the assembly:
   - *“What is one new perspective you learned today?”*
   - *“Did you feel comfortable sharing your views?” (1–5 scale)*
   - *“What should our community do next regarding AI?”*
2. **Facilitator Debrief (15 mins):** Gather your facilitators and notetakers immediately following the session to review what exercises generated the richest deliberation and where participants needed additional time.
3. **Closing the Loop:** Commit to emailing or mailing participants a summary of the final assembly outputs within two weeks, showing how their contributions have been shared locally and added to the PAVE Data Commons.
