The words used in invitations set the tone for your assembly. Effective invitations:

- **Avoid pre-judging whether AI is good or bad.** They invite critical inquiry rather than pitching enthusiasm or panic.
- **Focus on lived experience and community impacts**, rather than deep technical jargon or computer science concepts.
- **Explicitly emphasize that everyone can participate.** Reassure potential attendees that no technical background or prior knowledge is required.

---

## Example 1: Direct Invitation Template

Use this template when contacting specific community leaders, stakeholders, or targeted residents directly via email or letter:

```markdown
Subject: Invitation: Community Assembly on Artificial Intelligence in [Town / City / Community]

Dear [Name],

I am writing to invite you to take part in an upcoming Community Assembly on Artificial Intelligence (AI) taking place on [Date] at [Location / Venue].

In this gathering, we are bringing together people from across our community to learn, share everyday experiences, and reflect together on how AI is affecting our lives. We will discuss what AI technologies are currently doing in our neighbourhoods, workplaces, schools, and public services—and how we want technology to serve our community.

We are inviting a diverse group of residents and community voices who can bring different perspectives, and we would be delighted if you could join us. 

Over the course of the day (with regular breaks and refreshments provided), we will take time together to share our views and develop practical ideas for actions that can help our community respond to the changes AI may bring.

Key details:
• Date & Time: [Date, e.g. Saturday 14 November, 10:00 – 15:30]
• Venue: [Full Address, Accessibility Notes, e.g. wheelchair accessible, near central station]
• Refreshments: Lunch, coffee, and refreshments are provided free of charge.
• Support: [Include note on travel reimbursement, childcare stipends, or accessibility support].

You do not need any technical knowledge about AI to take part. The session is designed for everyone: whatever your prior experience or feelings about technology.

This assembly is organised by [Host Organisation] as part of a global network of community assemblies contributing to the Citizens’ Track on AI (CTAI). The insights and recommendations we develop together will be shared with CTAI, fed into international discussions, and presented directly to [local decision-makers].

Please let us know if you can attend by [RSVP Date] by replying to this email or registering at [Link].

Warm regards,

[Your Name / Organisation]
[Contact Information]
```

## Example 2: Open Call for Interest & Nomination Template

Use this template for posters, community newsletters, public noticeboards, or social media announcements:

```markdown
Artificial Intelligence is constantly in the headlines. But what do the people of [Town / City / Community] have to say about it?

On [Date], we are hosting a Community Assembly to talk about how AI is changing our daily lives—and how we, as a community, want to respond.

Everyone should have a say on the future of AI.

Join us to: Learn ➔ Discuss ➔ Act

• Date & Time: [Date, Time]
• Location: [Venue / Online link]
• Refreshments provided. Travel and caregiving support available.
• No technical background needed—all perspectives welcome.

Register your interest or nominate a neighbour to take part at [Registration Link] before [Deadline Date]. 

To ensure our assembly reflects the full diversity of our community, places are selected to represent a balanced mix of ages, backgrounds, and neighbourhoods.

Our community's recommendations will feed directly into local decision-makers and the Citizens’ Track on AI, a global initiative amplifying grassroots public voices in international AI governance.
```
