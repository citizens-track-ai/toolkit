## Question 1: Who are your going to invite and how?

An assembly could involve anything from 5 to 500 people and could last between 4 hours and 2 days. However, most community assemblies invite **15 to 50 participants** to come together for **one or two days**.

Remember: assemblies work best with diverse participation.

            <li>Intergenerational dialogue: Bring two existing groups together (e.g. youth club + older adults’ group) to foster new connections.</li>

          <ul style="margin: 0; padding-inline-start: 1.2rem;">

## Question 5: What Will Happen With the Outputs from Your Assembly?

Deliberation achieves true power when participants see concrete follow-through. Plan for three complementary output pathways:

<div class="guidance-grid-3">
  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border: 1px solid var(--ct-border-default, #DCD9CD); border-radius: var(--radius-md, 12px); padding: 1.5rem;">
    <h3 style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem; font-size: 1.15rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C);">1. Community Actions</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      Participants identify individual and collective initiatives. Support working groups to self-organise, hold follow-up meetings, and secure early "quick wins" that build local confidence and ongoing momentum.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border: 1px solid var(--ct-border-default, #DCD9CD); border-radius: var(--radius-md, 12px); padding: 1.5rem;">
    <h3 style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem; font-size: 1.15rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C);">2. PAVE &amp; Global Impact</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      Upload your assembly report and priority statements to Assemblis AI and the PAVE Data Commons. Your community will appear on the global map, and findings will be amplified in international policy forums.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div style="background: var(--ct-bg-surface-subtle, #F8F7F2); border: 1px solid var(--ct-border-default, #DCD9CD); border-radius: var(--radius-md, 12px); padding: 1.5rem;">
    <h3 style="margin: 0 0 0.5rem; font-size: 1.15rem; color: var(--ct-color-fjord, #00447C);">3. Targeted Local Policy</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0; font-size: 0.92rem; color: var(--color-slate, #4a5565); line-height: 1.55;">
      Present recommendations directly to local decision-makers: send joint letters to local councillors or MPs, submit evidence to parliamentary committees, or host a town-hall feedback briefing.
    </p>
  </div>
