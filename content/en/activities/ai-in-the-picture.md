## Purpose

This icebreaker activity has a number of overlapping goals. It should:

* **Create an environment in which everyone feels they can talk about their perspectives on AI.** Starting with artworks, rather than talk of particular digital technologies, can create a more level environment for people less confident in their own technical ability. 
* **Allows people to raise a wide range of issues**. Because of the images can invite a wide range of responses, they allow participants to bring up burning issues at the start of the session, and to have these heard: without going into deep discussion.
* **Introduce a range of positive and critical perspectives on AI.** Each image is accompanied by an artists statement that introduces particular perspectives on AI.

## Preparation

**For a physical workshop**

* Print the Better Images of AI collection (attached) in full colour
* Print double-sided to high quality card
* There should be _at least_ one card per person. For a group of 25 we recommend printing two full sets. 

If you cannot afford to print a full set of cards, you could also print a 'contact sheet' and then show full-size images on a projector. 

Place copies of the cards around the workshop room for people to find as they come in. 

**For an online workshop**

* Make the cards available to participants in a digital form. 

## Process & Steps

1. **As participants enter the workshop room invite them to choose an image that 'resonates' with them.** They should focus on the images on the front of the cards, not the artist statements at this point. 

2. **After, or as part of, a round of introductions, invite people to hold up and talk about the image they have chosen, and why they chose it.**

3. **Each image has a number: you can use the activity slide deck to pull up these images on a larger screen for everyone to see.** 

4. **(Optionally) Read or show the artists statement, and reflect on other themes raised by the image.** Note: participants may pick up very different themes from those the artist intended. There are no wrong answers! Validate what participants have said, and, if appropriate, introduce additional themes as a ['yes...and'](https://www.facilitating.xyz/chapter-6-unlocking-the-magic-of-facilitation-yes-and-rule/).
