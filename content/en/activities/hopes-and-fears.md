## Purpose

* Supporting an open discussion of hopes and fears for AI
* (Optionally) Inviting experts to join the discussion

## Preparation

You will need two-colours of post-it note to capture points from the discussion.

This activity can be run in two versions:

* **With outside experts** - where participants will take part in one-to-one or small-group conversations with AI researchers, scientists and policy makers;

* **With participants only** - where we use a 'think, pair, share' method to discuss hopes and fears in the group.

If you are inviting outside experts then you will need to provide them with a briefing, and make sure they are prepared to explain their work in an accessible way. 

## Process & Steps

This activity can be run in plenary with a small group (10 - 20 participants), or could be run at breakout tables with a larger group. 

* Ask the group to reflect on their **hopes** and **fears** for artificial intelligence
* Give participants two colours of post-it notes: one for hopes, and one for fears, and give them a few minutes to reflect and write-down at least one hope, and one fear

### With outside experts

* Split participants equally between the experts. Explain that _everyone_ has time to share their hopes and fears - not just the experts.
* The expert starts, and then go around everyone in turn, sharing hopes and fears written down on the post-it notes before. 
* After everyone has shared, open space for discussion. 
* If time allows, rotate experts between groups. 

### Participant-only version

* Participants form pairs and share their hopes and fears. As new themes emerge they write them down on post-it notes. 
* After a set interval (5 or 10 minutes) participants should switch pairs. 
* After a few rounds, bring the group back together to share their discussions. Collect post-it notes and cluster them around common themes. Pay attention to outliers as well.
