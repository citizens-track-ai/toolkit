The **United Nations Global Dialogue on AI Governance (UNGD)** is an ongoing international process bringing together multilateral representatives, grassroots civil society organizations, and AI developers.

### Core Focus Questions

1. **Global Priorities:** What international guardrails should the UN Global Dialogue prioritize over the coming biennium?
2. **Red Lines vs. Adaptive Standards:** Which AI issues demand non-negotiable legal red lines (e.g. lethal autonomous weapons, discriminatory algorithmic scoring in essential services), and which should follow adaptive voluntary standards?
3. **Equitable Global South Representation:** How can international AI governance mechanisms ensure developing nations and marginalized communities have binding decision-making power?

### How Citizens' Track Contributes

The Citizens’ Track on AI team synthesizes findings, deliberation cards, and recommendation declarations from participating community assemblies into an official civil society evidence submission presented directly to UN delegates.
