### About the PAVE Data Commons

PAVE stands for **Public AI Voice and Engagement**. The PAVE Data Commons is a resource that documents a wide range of participatory processes that have gathered community voices on AI.

If your assembly outcomes and recommendations are recorded in the PAVE Data Commons then they could:

- Feature on the PAVE map of engagement shown to decision-makers at key AI governance events around the world;
- Feed into policy briefs, consultation responses and reports highlighting public priorities for the future of AI;
- Form part of the evidence and advocacy work of the Citizens Track on AI.

---

### Questions and Outputs

**Scope:** Personal | Collective | National | Global

| Scope | Focus Questions & Prompts | Assembly Outputs & Artifacts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal** | How does AI impact my daily life, rights, and work? What immediate boundaries or skills do I need? | Personal reflections, participant feedback forms, individual priority checklists. |
| **Collective** | What do we want AI to do (or not do) in our local community, workplace, or schools? | Community resolutions, local action plans, community group working agreements. |
| **National** | What regulations, public investments, and accountability mechanisms should governments establish? | Policy recommendations, open letters to representatives, evidence for parliamentary inquiries. |
| **Global** | How should international bodies ensure equitable access, environmental sustainability, and human rights? | Submissions to multilateral consultations, international treaties feedback, global civil society briefs. |

---

### Three Deliberation Domains

#### 1. Values and Vision
What kind of society do we want to build with technology? What human values, rights, and cultural traditions must be preserved and prioritized above technological speed?

#### 2. Governance and Regulation
Who decides how AI models are built, trained, and deployed? What oversight, transparency, liability, and enforcement mechanisms are needed to hold tech providers accountable?

#### 3. Applications and Implementation
Where should AI be adopted, where should it be restricted or prohibited, and how can the economic and social benefits be shared fairly across all communities?
